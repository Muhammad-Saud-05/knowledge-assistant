# Retrieval evaluation report

Status: **Phase 1 baseline, development split only.** The nine holdout questions have not been run.
Generated by `python -m eval.run_eval --modes vector,keyword,hybrid --split dev --k 5 --candidates 30`.
Saved result: `eval/results/eval_20261114T083505Z_vector-keyword-hybrid_dev.json` (ranked chunk ids and
scores only, no document text).

## Setup

- Database: 30 documents, 502 chunks (4 original notes in `sample_corpus/`, 26 third-party documents
  whose sources and licences are in `docs/corpus_manifest.csv`).
- Embeddings: `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, 384 dimensions, cosine distance.
- Modes: **vector** (nearest chunks by meaning), **keyword** (PostgreSQL full-text search, OR semantics,
  `ts_rank_cd`), **hybrid** (30 candidates from each method fused with reciprocal rank fusion, k = 60).
  No reranker yet.
- Each question is searched in each mode with its filters, and the top 10 chunks are judged.
- Question file `eval/questions.json`, sha256 `68a66abfbb9e...`, repository commit `bf970ff`.

## The questions

40 questions over 24 documents (at most 3 questions per document, every `sample_corpus` note included):

| category | questions | notes |
|---|---|---|
| exact_term | 10 | the answer hinges on an identifier, flag, header or number; five are short keyword-style searches |
| paraphrase | 12 | worded differently from the source (at most one content word shared with the evidence) |
| multi_doc | 8 | each document contributes something the other lacks |
| unanswerable | 6 | three plausible-but-absent, one off-topic, two in-domain but absent |
| filtered | 4 | a file-type, a path-prefix and two date filters |

Nine questions are reserved as a **holdout** (every fourth question in each category); 31 are the
development set used here. Four single-document questions list an `also_acceptable` passage found by
text search (q07, q12, q20, q21). Each answerable question also stores its **lexical overlap** (the
share of its content words found in its best relevant passage) and a derived difficulty: `low_overlap`
at 0.25 or below, otherwise `high_overlap`. Across all 34 answerable questions, 8 are low-overlap and
26 are high-overlap. In the development set, among the 23 questions in the main table, 6 are low-overlap
and 17 are high-overlap.

The four filtered questions are **not** in the main results. They are a check that filters work and are
reported in their own table. The unanswerable questions have no retrieval metric; only their top-1
scores are recorded.

## Metric definitions

- A chunk is **relevant** if it comes from an expected document (or, for a single-document question,
  an `also_acceptable` one) and contains at least one of that document's evidence phrases, after
  normalisation (lowercase, whitespace collapsed, markdown emphasis characters and underscores removed,
  curly quotes made plain). This is strict and mechanical: another passage that also answers the
  question counts as a miss unless it was listed.
- **hit@k**: a relevant chunk in the top k. For a multi-document question this needs a relevant chunk
  from *every* expected document within the top k.
- **MRR@10**: reciprocal rank of the first relevant chunk in the top 10 (0 if none).
- **doc_hit@5**: any top-5 chunk from an expected document (the lenient measure: right document, not
  necessarily the right passage).
- **multi_doc coverage@5**: the share of a multi-document question's documents with a relevant chunk in
  the top 5, averaged over those questions.
- Counts are always shown next to percentages (for example 15/23). Intervals are percentile bootstrap
  95% intervals (1000 resamples, seed 7). The **paired** intervals resample the same questions for both
  modes and bootstrap the per-question difference, so they show whether a difference is real; two
  separate intervals that overlap cannot.
- Retrieval time is the time for one search call, including embedding the question, on this machine.

## What the data shows

Only what the numbers support, on 23 development questions in the main table (6 of them multi-document).

- **Hybrid beats keyword on this question set.** The paired difference is clear on both measures:
  hit@5 +0.261 (95% interval 0.04 to 0.48) and MRR@10 +0.288 (0.14 to 0.45). Keyword search missed
  14 of 23 questions at k=5 and had the lowest rank quality in every category.
- **Hybrid against vector is not settled.** Hybrid has the higher hit@5 (15/23 against 12/23, a paired
  difference of +0.130 with interval -0.04 to 0.30), but vector has the higher hit@1 (8/23 against
  6/23) and the higher MRR@10 (0.584 against 0.517; paired difference -0.067, interval -0.22 to 0.06).
  Neither interval excludes zero. The two modes differ on only 5 questions at hit@5 (4 for hybrid,
  1 for vector). In other words, hybrid finds a relevant chunk somewhere in the top 5 slightly more
  often, but it places it first less often. That is a case where hybrid is **worse than a single mode**
  on a rank-quality measure, though the evidence is too thin to call it real. By category the sign
  flips: hybrid has lower MRR@10 than vector on exact_term (0.483 against 0.603, 2 questions better
  and 3 worse) and multi_doc (0.503 against 0.667), and higher on paraphrase (0.556 against 0.511).
- **Retrieval usually finds the right document but not the right passage.** doc_hit@5 is 87.0% for
  vector and keyword and 95.7% for hybrid, while hit@5 is 52.2%, 39.1% and 65.2%. Reading the top
  results of hybrid's single-document misses (q01, q16, q19) showed chunks from the right document that
  are not the passage holding the answer; q20 returned unrelated documents.
- **Multi-document questions are hard for every mode.** hit@5 is 1/6 for vector, 0/6 for keyword and
  2/6 for hybrid. Four of the six (q23, q27, q28, q29) were missed by all three modes. Mean coverage@5
  is 0.42, 0.17 and 0.50.
- **The lexical-overlap buckets are small.** Only 6 development questions are low-overlap, so any
  bucket-level difference should be read with that in mind: for example, keyword reaches hit@5 on 3/6
  low-overlap questions against vector's 2/6, which is a difference of one question.
- **Filters work.** The three filtered development questions returned only documents satisfying their
  filters (0 violations) and all three were hit at k=5 in every mode.
- **Top-1 scores do not separate unanswerable from answerable questions in this sample.** Vector
  cosine for the five unanswerable development questions ranged from 0.720 to 0.792, inside the
  answerable range (0.652 to 0.894). Keyword and hybrid scores overlap in the same way. No threshold
  has been chosen.
- **Speed:** median search time per question was 67 ms for vector, 13 ms for keyword and 83 ms for
  hybrid (95th percentile 235, 33 and 204 ms).

## Limitations

- **Small sample.** 23 questions in the main table and 6 or fewer in many rows. The confidence
  intervals are wide, most differences between vector and hybrid are not distinguishable from zero,
  and changing a single question's label can move a percentage by several points.
- **Questions are not independent.** q15 and q23 (and q28) share passages: q15 and q23 both rest on
  the same passage of the HTTPS page, and q23 and q27 both use the Docker PDF's deployment advice.
  q28 is built from passages also covered, more generally, by the system-design primer. A single
  retrieval miss can therefore cost several questions at once, and the bootstrap, which resamples
  questions as if independent, understates the uncertainty.
- **Written for this corpus.** The questions were written against this specific, documentation-heavy
  corpus by the person who built the system, after reading the passages. Results may not carry over to
  other kinds of documents or to differently phrased questions.
- **Relevance is judged by evidence phrases.** It is mechanical and strict. Passages that answer a
  question but were not labelled (outside the four `also_acceptable` additions) count as misses, so
  hit rates are probably lower bounds. Word-level overlap is also only a rough proxy for how hard a
  question is.
- **Dates.** `doc_date` for the third-party documents is the file's modified date (2026-11-14), not a
  publication date. Only the author's own notes carry real front-matter dates, so the date-filter
  questions (q39 and q40) work only on those.
- **Only the development split has been run.** Any choice made from these tables, such as a reranker
  setting, risks fitting this small set. The holdout is for one final check with a frozen configuration.
- **Timing** comes from a single run on one machine and varies from run to run.
